Common questions

Why does the alarm ring again after I stop it?

That is the point. iOS always lets you stop an alarm from the Lock Screen, and no app can prevent it, so Snoozeless schedules follow-up alarms with every ring. Stop it and it rings again shortly, until you open Snoozeless and finish the mission. Tap the mission button on the alarm to go straight there.

How long it keeps coming back is the alarm's snooze-proof level:

  • Gentle: every minute, for 5 minutes.
  • Standard: every 30 seconds, then every 2 minutes, for 15 minutes.
  • Strict (Pro): as Standard, then every 5 minutes, for 30 minutes.
  • Relentless (Pro): as Standard, then every 5 minutes, for an hour.
How do I make sure it rings?
  1. Allow Alarms for Snoozeless when iOS asks, or later in Settings › Snoozeless.
  2. Turn the ringer volume up before bed. Alarms play at your ringer volume, and Apple doesn't let apps change it.
  3. Don't hide or lock Snoozeless with Face ID: iOS silences the alarms of hidden or locked apps.

Snoozeless doesn't need to stay open: the alarms belong to iOS and ring even if the app has been closed. It needs iOS 26 or later.

What are the wake-up missions?
  • Math: solve a few sums, at the difficulty you pick. Free.
  • Shake: shake your iPhone until the count is reached.
  • Steps: walk a number of steps.
  • Squats: do squats holding your iPhone.
  • Scan a code: scan the barcode or QR code you registered, for example on a product in the kitchen.
  • Photo: photograph the spot you registered, such as the bathroom sink.
  • Typing: retype a few sentences exactly; pasting is off.
  • Memory: tiles light up and go dark; tap them back.
  • Random: a different mission each time, from math, shake, steps, squats, typing and memory.

Tap Try this mission in the alarm editor to practise one before you rely on it.

The mission can't be finished. What now?

Every mission has a way out, so you are never stuck with a ringing phone. If the count stops moving, the registered code is gone, or the photo won't match after a few tries, the screen offers five hard math problems instead.

What is the wake-up check?

A Pro option per alarm. Three, five or ten minutes after you finish the mission, Snoozeless rings once more to make sure you didn't crawl back into bed. Hold the button to show you're up.

How do I pause alarms for a holiday?

Tap Pause on the alarm list and choose 1 day, 3 days, a week, or a date. Nothing rings until then; repeating alarms come back by themselves, and one-off alarms in between are skipped. In mainland China you can also set an alarm to repeat on workdays: public holidays off, make-up workdays (调休) on.

Nap, bedtime reminder, sleep sounds and the widget

Nap sets a one-time alarm a number of minutes from now and removes itself afterwards. The bedtime reminder sends a notification when it's time to sleep to get enough before your first alarm. Sleep sounds (rain, waves, a stream, crickets, a campfire and brown noise) keep playing with the screen locked and fade out on a timer; your alarm still rings. The widget shows your next alarm on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. All four are free.

How does the sleep report work?

Open Sleep, plug your iPhone in, leave it on the bedside table and tap Start sleeping. It listens through the night and stops when your next alarm rings or when you tap I'm awake (with no alarm set, after 12 hours). In the morning it shows how long the night was and what it heard: minutes of snoring, coughs, talking and other sounds. With Pro you also get the night as a timeline, the loudest snoring to play back, and your last nights side by side.

The sound is analysed on your iPhone and never leaves it; nothing listens unless you start it. The report doesn't show sleep stages: a phone on a bedside table can't measure them. Snoozeless is not a medical device and can't diagnose anything; if your snoring worries you, see a doctor.

What does Snoozeless Pro add, and how do I cancel?

Free: unlimited alarms with the math mission, the Gentle and Standard levels, nap, pause, sleep sounds, last night's sleep report, the bedtime reminder and the widget. Pro adds all nine missions, the Strict and Relentless levels, the wake-up check, the sleep report's timeline, snoring playback and history, more alarm sounds, colour themes and your wake-up history.

Pro is a yearly subscription (new subscribers get a free week first), a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The price in your currency is shown in the app before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription in the app. Cancelling during the free week costs nothing.

New iPhone? Tap Restore in Settings in the app, signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
