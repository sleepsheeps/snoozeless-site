Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Applies to Snoozeless: Alarm Clock for iPhone

Snoozeless collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no analytics. The only connection it makes is to Apple's App Store, for Snoozeless Pro. Your alarms, photos, mornings and nights stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Stored inside the app, on your iPhone only: your alarms (times, days, labels, missions, sounds), the code you register for the scan mission, the photo you take of your wake-up spot, your wake-up history (when each alarm rang and when you finished the mission, the last 500), your settings, and the date your Pro subscription runs until, so Pro keeps working offline.

If you use the sleep report: for each night, when it started and ended and, minute by minute, how many seconds of snoring and talking and how many coughs were heard and how loud it was. With Snoozeless Pro, also the eight loudest 15-second clips of snoring, so you can play them back; without Pro no clips are recorded. A night's minutes and clips are kept for the 7 most recent nights, its totals for 30. Deleting a night deletes its clips.

The alarms themselves are scheduled with iOS, which rings them. The widget reads a copy of your next alarms from a storage area shared between the app and its widget on the same iPhone.

We never receive any of it. Deleting an alarm deletes its photo; deleting Snoozeless deletes everything. Like any app's data, it is part of your own iPhone backups (iCloud or a computer) if you have them on, each night's totals included; the sleep report's minute-by-minute details and its snoring clips are left out of backups.

Permissions

Alarms

Needed so iOS lets Snoozeless set real alarms that ring on silent and through Focus.

Camera

Only for the scan and photo missions, when you register a code or a spot and when you scan or photograph it to stop the alarm. Codes are read on your iPhone, and photos are compared there with Apple's Vision framework. Only the registered code and the photo you registered are kept; the photo taken each morning is deleted as soon as it has been compared. Snoozeless never records video.

Microphone

Only for the sleep report, and only from when you tap Start sleeping until your next alarm rings or you tap I'm awake, whichever comes first; with no alarm set, it stops by itself after 12 hours. During that time the sound is analysed on your iPhone, by Apple's built-in sound recognition, to count snoring, coughs and talking. It is not recorded, except for the short snoring clips described above (Pro only), which stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing listens when the sleep report is off.

Motion & Fitness

Only for the steps mission, to count steps while it is on screen. Shake and squat missions use the motion sensors, which need no permission. Counts are used live and not stored.

Notifications

Only if you turn on the bedtime reminder. It is scheduled on your iPhone; there are no push notifications.

Photos (add only)

Only when you choose Save Image for your wake-up card: iOS adds that picture to your library. Snoozeless cannot see your photos.

When you share

The wake-up card is drawn on your iPhone and handed to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, under that app's own privacy policy.

Snoozeless Pro and the App Store

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple. To show prices, to buy or restore, and to check whether a subscription is still active, the app connects to the App Store. Snoozeless never sees your payment details or Apple ID; Apple gives developers only anonymous sales totals. The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt; iOS decides whether it appears.

Children

Snoozeless collects no data from anyone, including children.
